# Global SME Regional Settings

Traxium's customer-facing interface remains in English. Workspaces can choose a number and date display locale, an IANA time zone for actual event timestamps, metric or US customary unit suggestions, a default card currency, and a reporting currency independently. The supported financial currencies are **USD and EUR only**. Country is recorded explicitly on a pilot request and does not set currency or units.

Admins set these choices in **Settings → Workspace**. New workspace setup suggests the browser locale and time zone and starts with metric suggestions; users confirm or change them before submitting. An `Intl` locale code such as `en-US`, `de-DE`, or `ja-JP` controls display punctuation and date labels, not interface translation. Impact dates are calendar dates and stay on the same date across time zones; actual timestamps are displayed in the workspace time zone with daylight-saving transitions. APIs and stored date-only values use ISO dates.

The workspace unit preference changes suggestions only. Every card, quote, forecast, and actual keeps its explicit unit. Price-per-unit and annual volume must use the same basis. The shared catalog supports each/pieces; g, kg, metric tonnes, lb, US short tons; mL, L, US gallons, imperial gallons; metres and feet; plus packs, pallets, hours, and services. `ton` and `gallon` alone are ambiguous. Compatible physical units have defined conversion factors. When a supplier or material quote is applied, its unit price converts to the card's unit without changing the card quantity or monetary savings basis. Business units and custom units have no invented cross-unit conversion; an identical custom unit can be retained. Historical quotes without a recorded unit must be resolved explicitly before application. Finance-locked card values cannot be changed by applying a quote.

Saving cards calculate `(baseline price − new price) × annual volume` in their own currency. Each EUR card stores its EUR-to-USD rate, where the value means **USD received for one EUR**, and may store its effective date and source. USD cards use rate 1. Historical EUR records without a recorded rate date remain undated; their stored USD amounts are left intact. New card forms request a date when entering a manual EUR rate. Workspace portfolio totals use stored USD audit amounts, then translate once to EUR at the latest positive EUR-to-USD rate effective at the reporting date when EUR reporting is selected. Missing or invalid rates block EUR totals or exports with an actionable message. Changing reporting currency does not alter card currencies, finance approvals, locked values, or historical FX. Existing fiscal-year rules continue to apply.

Saving-card XLSX imports retain the older column aliases. For new workbooks, use explicit `Currency`, `Volume Unit`, and `FX Rate (EUR to USD)` columns; include `FX Rate Effective Date` on EUR rows. Choose a dot or comma decimal convention before import, and omit grouping separators in numeric text. Dates must be Excel dates or ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. Imports reject unsupported currencies, ambiguous units, malformed numbers, and ambiguous dates with row-level errors. The controller workbook includes numeric financial cells, explicit local and reporting currencies, the saved FX basis, ISO-formatted date cells, and USD audit values. The import template includes USD/EUR and metric/US customary examples. Volume CSV import has explicit separator and decimal choices and records the card's unit.

Apply the additive migration `20261005190000_global_sme_preferences` with the normal deployment migration process. It adds workspace preferences, card FX metadata, quote units, and price precision, and removes the default country on *new* pilot leads. It does not rewrite historical leads or card values. Existing workspaces retain USD reporting, `en-US` display, UTC time zone, and metric suggestions until an admin changes them. Do not reset the database or rewrite applied migrations.

Procurement reporting currency is separate from Stripe subscription currency. Existing Stripe subscriptions and price IDs are not changed. Checkout verifies that the configured base and optional metered Prices both use USD or EUR and the same currency; unavailable or mismatched provider prices fail clearly. To sell a different subscription currency, configure real matching Stripe Product/Price IDs and review billing operations first. Traxium does not fabricate EUR prices or map USD IDs to EUR. This product change does not assert provider coverage, local tax support, certifications, data residency, or legal compliance in any country.
